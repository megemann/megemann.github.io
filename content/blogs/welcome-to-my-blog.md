## Hello World!

Welcome to my technical blog! This space is where I'll be sharing my thoughts, experiences, and insights about the fascinating world of AI and data.

I created this blog with two main purposes in mind: to deepen my own understanding through teaching and to help simplify machine learning concepts for others. From personal experience, I found there was a large lack of resources to really grasp 'what is machine learning' when you're just getting into the space. Additionally, I hope to share what I have worked on to show how these simple concepts and tools can be applied in a real-world setting.

What really excites me about this field is how rapidly it's evolving. Every day brings new breakthroughs, techniques, and applications that push the boundaries of what's possible. My passion lies in exploring these frontiers and finding practical ways to apply these innovations to real-world problems.

**What makes this blog different?** I'm approaching these topics from the perspective of both a student and a practitioner. I'll be learning alongside you, documenting my journey, mistakes and all, while also sharing practical insights from my research and projects to make sure that your experience is - well a little more smooth. I believe this balance of theoretical understanding and hands-on experience fills a large gap in the machine learning space and provides a different perspective on how to view the field.

I'd love for this to be a two-way conversation! Please don't hesitate to leave comments, ask questions, or suggest topics you'd like me to cover. Your engagement will help shape the content and create a community of like-minded learners and practitioners. For a more personal engagement with me, please go to my X: @ajfairbanksML

In the coming months, I'll be launching a series on hyperparameter optimization (a research area I'm particularly passionate about), breaking down influential research papers, and sharing tutorials on building practical ML applications. Stay tuned!

## About Me

Hello! My name is Austin Fairbanks, and I am a student at the University of Massachusetts, Undergrad Class of '26 and Masters Class of '27. I am studying Computer Science, with a Concentration in Data Science and Machine Learning. My passion lies in Pythonic Machine Learning, having experience with many major machine learning and data science frameworks such as PyTorch, TensorFlow, Pandas, NumPy, etc. Outside of School, I thoroughly enjoy going to the gym, playing a few rounds of golf, and reading!

### My Technical Journey

Although my technical journey is short, it has been full of meaningful experiences. In high school, I got introduced to Computer Science through My FIRST Tech Challenge Robotics team, being one of the lead programmers specializing in Autonomous Navigation. Once reaching college, I self-taught skills in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to try out web development, so I would have a creative way to display my projects. Then in my first summer in college, I had the amazing opportunity to work on Machine Learning on contract at Corning Incorporated, which ignited my passion for the industry. After this internship, I have strived to learn as much as I can, creating many projects and practicing with different machine learning techniques. With many more experiences on the horizon, and an internship quickly approaching, I look forward to what the future holds for me.

### Areas of Interest

Specifically for me, I am interested in developing concrete methods to make high-performing models, as well as manipulating data of various kinds to provide technical insights. Firstly, in the automated hyperparameter tuning industry, I have seen a large gap in the documentation surrounding the effectiveness in certain use-cases of the algorithms provided to tune models. I hope to bridge this gap in my self-study in Spring 2026 by creating a systematic way to evaluate algorithms on certain classifications of problems. Additionally, I enjoy extracting data from different sectors, and learning the ins and outs to make informed decisions based upon this data. My first project about this is through RateMyProfessors data, in which I hope to inform fellow students about what teachers they should pick.

With each blog I write, I hope to keep constantly improving and continue reaching more and more people.
